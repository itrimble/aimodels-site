Bosgame M5: one box vs two

One M5 is a Ryzen AI Max+ 395 with 128 GB of unified LPDDR5X at about 210-220 GB/s measured. On Linux about 108-110 GB is usable by the GPU (96 GB cap on Windows). Two boxes linked with llama.cpp RPC give roughly 200-225 GB, which adds capacity, not speed. Mixture-of-experts models are the sweet spot because only the active experts are read per token.

Headlines

  • One M5 runs Qwen3.8 Flash-Next (125B MoE, 6B active) at IQ4_XS or alesha's abliterated IQ3_S, and GLM-5.3-Flash (320B, 18B active) at 2-bit with little headroom.
  • Two M5s add the Flash-Next Q4_K_XL abliterated build and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at IQ1_M (ISTA-DASLab build; the 110 GB Engram table stays memory-mapped on SSD).
  • Not even two M5s hold the huihui DeepSeek IQ1_M build fully resident (~268 GB est.) or any original safetensors weights; those are archive copies to quantize from.
  • The MacBook Pro (36 GB) runs Qwen3.8-27B at Q4 or the 2.4-bit Mirai build, plus all speech and music models.
  • Video: Wan 2.2 A14B needs the card's offload flags even on an M5; HunyuanVideo 1.5 (8.3B) is the lighter video option.

Two engines matter: alesha-pro's Mirai and Maya builds need their own forked engines (mainline llama.cpp, LM Studio and Ollama will not load them), and Maya/Strata builds can't be split across two boxes with llama.cpp RPC.
