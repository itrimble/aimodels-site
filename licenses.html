License names come from each repo's Hugging Face card metadata. This is a summary, not legal advice: read the actual license before shipping anything.

Non-commercial: Qwen-Image 2.1 (Qwen Research License), YuE2 (CC BY-NC 4.0) and Fish Audio S2 Pro (Fish Audio Research License) can't be used in anything sold without a separate license. HunyuanVideo 1.5's license doesn't apply in the EU, UK or South Korea.

Abliterated models

Abliterated builds (huihui-ai, alesha-pro) have had refusal behaviour removed or suppressed by third parties, either baked into the weights or applied with a control vector at run time. They inherit the base model's license. They will answer requests that the original would refuse, so:

  • Use them for lawful, authorised work only: defensive research, testing your own systems, red-team prep with written scope.
  • Don't expose them on a public endpoint or share access.
  • Review outputs before acting on them; you are responsible for what you do with them.
  • They are community conversions, not official releases, and their quality can differ from the original.
