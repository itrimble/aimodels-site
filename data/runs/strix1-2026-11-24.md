# strix1 benchmark receipts, 23-24 November 2026

Our first Bosgame M5 (strix1), every GGUF model in the archive that runs in llama.cpp, on both of llama.cpp's GPU backends
(ROCm/HIP and Vulkan/RADV), same build (b12670), same plan, back to back: 512- and 8,192-token prompts, 128 generated
tokens from an empty context and at 8,192 tokens of depth, 3 repeats, median shown with min-max. Each model's weights are
identified by SHA-256 and were verified byte for byte against the NAS archive after copying.

Not here: Mirai S 27B (its engine is CUDA only; it does not run on this GPU), the huihui BF16 (used only to make the Q6_K
below), image, speech and music models (not llama.cpp LLMs). The Strata Flash-Next IQ3_S is run in stock llama.cpp, not
in the Strata engine its card recommends. Power is the driver-reported socket power (CPU + GPU), not a wall meter. Room
temperature was not recorded. The runs crossed midnight, so they come from two dated folders on strix1.

Superseded: the earlier receipt for 23 November (strix1-2026-11-23.md) used the mean of 3 and had no decode at depth.
